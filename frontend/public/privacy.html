Privacy Policy

MCM Lift Tracker · Last updated November 13, 2026

What this app is

MCM Lift Tracker is a personal, single-user workout tracker. It is not offered to the public. Only its owner can sign in.

Data it collects

  • Workout logs the owner enters: dates, exercises, weights, reps and effort ratings.
  • With the owner's permission, data from the owner's WHOOP account through the official WHOOP API: recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, strain and step count.

How it is used

The data is used only to show the owner their own training history and recovery, and to run statistical models on it inside the app. It is not used for advertising.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties. It is stored in a private database used only by this app, and the app is reachable only after signing in.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. The owner can disconnect WHOOP at any time, which deletes the stored WHOOP access tokens; access can also be revoked from the WHOOP app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's GitHub repository.
